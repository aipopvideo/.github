# AI Pop Video

Turn your photos into short AI dance videos and creative effects.

**[Try it in your browser →](https://aipopvideo.com/)**

## What you can make
- Dance and effect templates: [AI Dance Generator](https://aipopvideo.com/ai-dance-generator) · [all templates](https://aipopvideo.com/templates)
- Two-photo scenes like [Hotel Lobby AI](https://aipopvideo.com/hotel-lobby-ai-video-generator)
- Your own moves: upload a reference video plus a photo and the AI copies the motion
- Small tools: [Video Merger](https://aipopvideo.com/tools/video-merger) · [Depth Video Converter](https://aipopvideo.com/tools/depth-video)
